# N45 immutable atomic 1 GiB source leaves

N45 separately opts into immutable WB/IPAT1/AD1 EPT12 1 GiB PDPTE leaves.
The composed EPT02 cache still consists entirely of private 4 KiB leaves.
N44's 2 MiB opt-in and all older APIs remain selectable and retain their
original rejection policy. This is not support for guest structural writes,
live downgrades/unmaps, source replacement, reclamation, arbitrary memory
types, DMA, other VMs, or native huge EPT02 mappings.

## Production policy and translation

`nested_ept_atomic_walk_wb_ipat_1g` and `nrc_init_atomic_wb_1g` accept 4 KiB,
2 MiB and 1 GiB source leaves. The shared format decoder accepts PDPTE PS
only at depth 1 with explicit 1 GiB policy and at least 30 physical address
bits. Bits 29:12 of the base must be zero; high address bits, unsupported
fields, invalid permissions, memory types and missing IPAT are rejected.
PML4 and PT PS remain unsupported. Nonpresent payload is never decoded.

The full 30-bit source offset is applied before selecting and authorizing a
4 KiB backing page. Each selected page can have a distinct HPA or permissions;
other offsets may be holes. Neither a whole-GiB allocation nor HPA contiguity
is required or inferred. Source format failures remain unsupported rather
than architectural reason 49. Present-path L0 backing failure takes precedence
over L1 permission denial; a genuine L1 denial remains the reason-48 path.

Only the separate N45 profile advertises virtual EPT capabilities 16 and 17.
Physical EPT02 huge-page support is not needed. The existing physical EPT A/D,
WB and invalidation capability requirements continue to apply.

## Atomic source lifetime and private tracking

Every source-table word is accessed atomically. The caller retains immutable
structure, source identity, pinned writable WB host authority, and proof that
all guest aliases of source tables are read-only through every source lease
and private-cache lifetime. Detection of structural corruption fails closed;
it is not a racing source-update protocol.

The actual PDPTE terminal is recorded at depth 1, leaf shift 30, in a two-entry
trace. Fresh permits revalidate exact source/cache/A-D identities, entry GPA,
table HPA, pointer, raw structure, depth and terminal position. Forged short
nonleaf or long huge paths cannot authorize promotion. A/D compare-and-swap
preserves structural fields and never replays a stale observed D on a read.

Shared source D does not grant private write permission. Every newly read-filled
writable 4 KiB EPT02 mapping still traps on its own first write, including far
subpages and another owner. An allowed write sets D at the actual terminal;
a legal source permission denial sets only A.

## Stopped-owner maintenance

TAM visits every registered source table at a consistent depth and handles
mixed 4 KiB, 2 MiB and 1 GiB roots. PDPTE PS terminates the graph and permits
clearing both A and D. It does not require resolving all 262,144 backing pages.
Nonpresent payload remains unchanged, and every registered table still must
be reached. The full owner inventory, stopped physical consumers, updater
drain, physical-reference removal, exact invalidation receipts, private-cache
reset, source audit and final stopped-owner validation remain mandatory.

## Bounded real-guest profile

Build `make -C efi smp-nested-atomic-gigapage` after sourcing
`efi/scripts/cloud-env.sh`. The separate profile retains the low 4 KiB source
subtree and uses sparse actual guest accesses at 1 GiB-relative offsets
0xf000, 0x200000 and 0x3ffff000. Those accesses span a 2 MiB boundary and reach
the last 4 KiB of the source GiB. All high L1 backing is also represented by
actual sparse 4 KiB EPT01 mappings and audited along with retained low aliases.
See [profile layout and limits](tests/N45_PROFILE.md).

Two fixed physical owners execute the retained two-reset/13-checkpoint
protocol. A/B share the huge source leaf and C uses a 4 KiB alternate mapping.
The test checks each private target leaf, durable source identity and reset
receipt rather than inferring success from a shared source D bit. It does not
execute malformed source, hole or readonly-backing negative tests in hardware.

## Reproducible focused software gates

- `bash efi/tests/nested45.sh --host`: focused production core, ownership,
  independent review, integration and fresh freestanding/ELF-ESP checks.
- `python3 efi/tests/nested45_core.py --sanitizer none`
- `python3 efi/tests/nested45_core.py --sanitizer address,undefined`
- `python3 efi/tests/nested45_core.py --sanitizer thread`
- `bash efi/tests/nested45.sh --runtime`: two-vCPU TCG capability check only.

The focused core runs 23 cases per mode using production walker/catalog/typed
owner/TAM code with mocked physical primitives. It covers nonlinear HPAs,
both sides of a 2 MiB boundary, the final source page, readonly backing and
holes, mixed depths, per-owner private first writes, two clear/retrack rounds,
trace corruption, malformed source and retained failure paths. The independent
gate runs 15 groups in O2/Werror, ASan/UBSan and TSan, rejects 25 weakening
mutants, checks all 262,144 nonlinear-backed subpage offsets, and forces 100
shared-PDPTE CAS conflicts per mode. It also checks constructor fail-atomicity,
durable trace attacks, mixed-depth maintenance and the physical-fixture ownership
audit. Deterministically forced host CAS conflicts and TSan are software
evidence, not proof of hardware CAS collisions.

A successful freestanding build proves compilation/linking only. A TCG
CPUID_VMX0 skip does not prove a nested guest ran. Full software regression,
exact-image hardware execution and its receipt/audit evidence remain separate
acceptance gates; do not claim them from these focused checks.
